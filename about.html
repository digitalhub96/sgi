Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Sree Grand Inn located?

Sree Grand Inn is centrally located near by famous MAHAMAHAM TANK in Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu, near all major temples and tourist attractions, making it a convenient stay for pilgrims and tourists alike.

What room types are available at Sree Grand Inn?

We offer a range of accommodations including Premium Rooms, Classic Rooms, Superior Rooms, and Executive Suites, all designed to provide comfort and convenience.

Does the hotel offer complimentary Wi-Fi?

Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel for all guests.

Is parking available at the hotel?

Yes, we provide free parking for our guests, ensuring your vehicle is safe during your stay.

Are Navagraha temple tour arrangements available?

Yes, we provide Navagraha temple tour guide arrangements and cab services to make your pilgrimage seamless and comfortable.

Are there nearby tourist attractions?

Yes, the hotel is close to famous temples such as Adi Kumbeswarar Temple, Sarangapani Temple, and Mahamaham Tank, as well as other cultural landmarks.

Does the hotel have a 24-hour front desk?

Yes, our front desk is operational 24/7 to assist you with any requests or inquiries during your stay.

How do I make a reservation at Sree Grand Inn?

You can make a reservation by calling our hotel directly, booking through our website, or using popular online travel platforms.
