No Refund Policy

All bookings and purchases made with Sree Grand Inn are non-refundable. Once a reservation is confirmed, it cannot be canceled or modified, and no refunds will be issued under any circumstances, including but not limited to early departures, late arrivals, or weather-related issues. We recommend double-checking your booking details before finalizing your reservation. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us directly.
